Tourist Destinations

1. MANALI

Manali is a beautiful hill station in Himachal Pradesh.

Famous for: Snowfall, mountains, and adventure sports.

2. GOA

Goa is a popular tourist destination known for its beaches.

Famous for: Beaches, water sports, and sunsets.

3. JAIPUR

Jaipur is the capital of Rajasthan and is known as the Pink City.

Famous for: Hawa Mahal, forts, palaces, and culture.
